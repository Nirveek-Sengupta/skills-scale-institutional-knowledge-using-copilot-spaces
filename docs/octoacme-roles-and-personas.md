# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Leads align cross-functional teams, make prioritization decisions, and ensure initiatives deliver customer and business value. They sit above Product Managers and Project Managers to provide strategic oversight and resolve trade-offs across multiple initiatives.

### Responsibilities
- Set product vision and strategy across the portfolio
- Make final prioritization and trade-off decisions between competing initiatives
- Align stakeholders on roadmap, releases, and strategic direction
- Escalate risks and dependencies across teams
- Review outcomes and metrics post-launch
- Support Product Managers in stakeholder management and high-level decisions

### Goals
- Ensure product strategy drives all initiatives and maximizes ROI
- Reduce cross-team blockers through proactive alignment
- Maximize customer and business value across the product portfolio

### Typical Communication
- Weekly PM + PdM syncs for strategic alignment
- Stakeholder reviews and roadmap presentations
- Risk and dependency escalations to executive sponsors
- Post-release outcome reviews and strategy adjustments

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Works with Product Managers**: Provides strategic direction; Product Managers implement the tactical roadmap
- **Works with Project Managers**: Resolves high-level blockers and dependencies; Project Managers manage day-to-day execution
- **Works with Developers**: Reviews technical feasibility of strategic initiatives; Developers propose solutions aligned with strategy

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality strategies, validate acceptance criteria, and ensure features meet quality standards before release. They champion quality throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and test plans for features and releases
- Validate that acceptance criteria are clear and testable during planning
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts across the team
- Validate features against acceptance criteria before handoff to production
- Identify and triage quality issues and regressions
- Advocate for adequate testing time in sprint planning
- Define and maintain quality metrics and standards

### Goals
- Ensure all releases meet quality standards
- Reduce production defects and regressions
- Provide clear quality signals to the team and stakeholders
- Shift quality left by catching issues early in the development cycle

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria refinement
- Test result summaries and quality reports
- QA sign-off on features ready for release
- Post-release verification and regression testing
- Quality dashboards and metrics reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Collaborates on test strategy and early testing; validates code meets acceptance criteria
- **Works with Product Managers**: Refines acceptance criteria to ensure they are testable; reports quality status
- **Works with Project Managers**: Communicates testing timelines and quality risks; escalates blockers

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Officers ensure that projects meet security standards, compliance requirements, and organizational policies. They mitigate security risks and ensure regulatory adherence throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define and enforce security and compliance requirements for projects
- Conduct security reviews and threat assessments for new features
- Ensure compliance with industry standards and regulatory requirements
- Coordinate security incident response and post-incident reviews
- Manage security scanning and vulnerability assessments in CI/CD pipelines
- Provide guidance on secure coding practices and architecture decisions
- Escalate security and compliance risks to leadership

### Goals
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and compliance violations in releases
- Embed security best practices in the development lifecycle
- Ensure regulatory and organizational compliance
- Enable rapid, secure incident response

### Typical Communication
- Security review gates in release planning
- Vulnerability assessments and risk reports
- Security incident communications and post-mortems
- Compliance audits and audit reports
- Security training and guidance to teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Provides secure coding guidance; reviews technical implementations for security risks
- **Works with Project Managers**: Escalates security and compliance blockers; participates in risk management
- **Works with Product Leads**: Ensures security requirements are factored into strategic decisions

---

## Sponsor/Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors/Executive Stakeholders provide business context, approve resources, and ensure projects align with organizational strategy. They are the primary decision-makers for business-level trade-offs and escalations.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic alignment for initiatives
- Approve resource allocation and budget for projects
- Make or escalate business-level trade-off decisions
- Review and approve high-level project plans and timelines
- Monitor project health and receive executive status reports
- Remove organizational blockers and dependencies
- Advocate for the project's success across the organization

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Align project execution with organizational strategy
- Remove executive-level blockers
- Maximize return on investment (ROI)

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or quarterly executive status updates
- Decision gates for project approval and funding
- Escalation points for business-level blockers
- Outcome reviews and post-project retrospectives
- Strategic alignment meetings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Works with Project Managers**: Reviews project status and removes high-level blockers
- **Works with Product Leads**: Aligns on strategic direction and resolves cross-portfolio trade-offs
- **Works with Product Managers**: Provides business context and success metrics

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define technical strategy, system design, and technology decisions for projects. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with technical standards and organizational strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical strategy and system architecture for initiatives
- Make technology decisions (frameworks, platforms, libraries)
- Ensure scalability, performance, and maintainability of solutions
- Conduct technical feasibility assessments and design reviews
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Provide technical guidance and mentorship to developers
- Ensure solutions align with organizational technical standards and patterns

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable, and maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable rapid, confident feature delivery
- Build reusable components and patterns

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technology selection and evaluation reports
- Technical risk assessments and mitigation plans
- Code review guidance and technical mentoring
- Architecture documentation and standards

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Provides technical guidance and design direction; reviews implementations for alignment
- **Works with Project Managers**: Escalates technical risks and dependencies; manages architecture work in project plans
- **Works with Product Managers**: Advises on technical feasibility of features; identifies technical constraints
